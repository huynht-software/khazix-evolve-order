How to read this

Lobby-type, jungle, and champion bars show the majority first evolve from the left; gray is the rest. They are sorted by how far from 50/50 the split is, either way. Click a lobby or jungle type on a chart or match card to filter games. Champion rows need at least three games.

Each card is evolve order, lobby tags, then the enemy team (jungle outlined). Open Kit & notes for body / move / fight roles, CC, reveals, and a short composition writeup. Body is tank, bruiser, or squishy. Move is mobile or immobile. Fight is peel, engage, and/or self-peel. Hard CC locks you; soft CC is slow, blind, silence, and the like.

Above the cards: Q first, R first, or Twists. Twists are games where a simple model (R only vs early targeted threats, and only if the jungler cannot just reveal him) disagrees with what he evolved.

Q evolve

Isolation damage — the usual first evolve in this sample. Invis is easy to waste into reveals, tanks he can just jump on, or a lobby that is mostly skillshots.

R evolve

A third invis cast. Worth more when the threat is targeted spells or autos and nobody reveals him. Cassio, Nautilus, and some ADCs often still get R later rather than first.
